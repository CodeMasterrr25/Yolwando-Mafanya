About me

Not a developer. A problem solver.

I am Yolwando Mafanya, a digital systems architect, data analyst, and AI solutions specialist based in Johannesburg, South Africa. I work at the intersection of operations, compliance, and technology, building tools that give business leaders the visibility and control they are missing.

My background spans pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality assurance, production coordination, and regulatory compliance, giving me a rare understanding of how complex, regulated organisations actually function. That means the systems I build are not academic exercises. They are designed by someone who understands your operational reality.

I have designed and delivered everything from 15-module quality management systems to AI-powered operations dashboards used daily by general managers and regulatory teams. I work with business owners, operations managers, property investors, and compliance professionals who need systems that solve specific problems, not generic software that creates new ones.
