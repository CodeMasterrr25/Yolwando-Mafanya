Products

Three products that work as one system.

Most companies buy a tracker, a document tool and a reporting tool, then pay people to keep them in sync. Mine are built to hand work to each other.

Aura

In testing on site

The AI assistant for quality and production teams. It writes, reads and listens.

  • Drafts SOPs, deviations and CAPAs in minutes, matched to your own templates
  • Turns meeting transcripts into owned, dated actions
  • Reads filled forms with OCR, so paper records become data
  • Offers follow-on outputs, such as training slides from a new procedure
  • Runs on open-source models or OpenAI, password protected, inside your network

Built for QA, validation and production QMS work.

Nexus

In use on site

The command platform that tracks every quality commitment across departments.

  • CAPAs traced to the client or regulator that raised them
  • Validation tracked per person, rolled up into one site view
  • Change controls and quality agreements in the same place
  • Role-scoped logins: process owners see their work, executives see everything
  • Audit trail on every change, with overdue alerts and escalation

Used by a CEO, a Responsible Pharmacist and a Supply Chain Director.

Executive reporting

A board brief, not a dashboard.

Executives do not want forty charts. They want to know what changed, what it costs and what they need to decide. Aura writes this brief straight from Nexus data, so it is never stale and never massaged.

  • Decisions firstThe page opens with what needs a yes or no, and what it costs to wait.
  • Plain languageOne paragraph a CEO can read in thirty seconds, written from the live numbers.
  • Every number has a sourceEach figure links back to the record in Nexus, so the audit trail survives the board meeting.
  • Trend over snapshotEach indicator shows its direction, so a bad month and a bad trend are never confused.
